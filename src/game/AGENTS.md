## Game rules
- `enemy-fragments.ts` owns deterministic melee sprite cuts and lethal-third/critical shattering; engine crops actual pose pixels, caches cut frames, and reuses world gib physics/depth rendering. Why: visible damage never consumes combat dice or alters HP, loot or AI.
- Projectile barrel hits use swept, height-aware collision before wall responses in engine.ts; explosion-sprites.ts supplies deterministic fire/smoke animation. Why: fast shots cannot skip barrels and visual effects never change explosion damage.
- Floor enemy HP, elite chance and base damage use `progression.ts`; enemy evolution (threat from floor, kills and player level → HP/damage/speed and unlocked traits) lives in `evolution.ts`, covered by `evolution.test.ts`. Why: enemies grow progressively in one tested place.
- Camera angle projection and exponential input smoothing use `camera.ts`; player projectile lifetime derives from weapon reach. Why: vertical aiming, world surfaces and longer shots share consistent rules.
- Zone variants are defined in `alterations.ts`, while gate groups, raids and timed boss denial remain simulation state in engine.ts. Why: presentation reads effects without changing combat or access rules.
- Stealth/lock/legacy rules live in engine.ts (`aware`/`notice` per enemy, `forceRoll`, `legacy` in localStorage `ars_anima_legacy`) and are covered by `stealth.test.ts`.
- `terrain.ts` loads bundled ground materials; `groundKind` selects room ground and connecting paths without changing geometry. Why: dirt, sand and paving remain presentation-only surfaces.
- Prop break outcomes (sturdy/reward/splinters/scatter), splinter values and per-level stat growth live in `world-rules.ts` (`propBreakOutcome`, `SPLINTER`, `levelBonus`) and are covered by `ambush-loot.test.ts`. Why: tuning stays in one tested place.
- Ambushers are placed by `placeAmbusher` (ceiling, balcony, window, pillar, chandelier, dark corner, rubble, behind-door); once triggered they clear `ambush` and use normal AI. The map tab reads only `roomIntel()`.
- Enemy poses `rest`/`alert`/`down` come from `animation.ts`; `downT`/`alertT` on enemies drive knockdowns (slide/kick cancel attacks) and the startle reaction. Sanity episodes surface only via `snapshot().sanityFx` CSS classes. HUD art is silhouette-cut (outer background removed, interior holes refilled) from the user boards (`hud-art.ts`) and only appears, pulses or fades in response to snapshot state.
- Kill-streak frenzy, enemy-miss openings (×2), survival needs/fatigue, lumbre rest regen, mirror buffs and randomized class attributes are tuned in `momentum.ts` (tested in `momentum.test.ts`); engine reads them. Why: one tested place for momentum and rest balance.
- Hit-and-run retreat tuning lives in `RETREAT`/`retreatChance` in `combat-fx.ts` (tested in `combat-fx.test.ts`); engine rolls it in `damageEnemy` and moves the enemy away during `retreatT`. Why: post-hit withdrawal balance stays in one tested place.
- Melee hack-and-slash chains (steps, pull/hold, lunge, per-weapon finisher) live in pure `melee-combo.ts` (tested in `melee-combo*.test.ts`); engine only reads them in the swing block. Why: combo feel is tuned in one tested place.
- Perfect-block window/taken/reflect multipliers live in `PERFECT_BLOCK` in combat-fx.ts; retreat cue toggles/volume/size/intensity live in `Game.retreatCue` (presentation only, persisted by GameView).

- Knockback body impacts (wall slams/bounces, enemy-to-enemy chains, downed bonus) are tuned in `impact.ts` (tested in `impact.test.ts`); engine `launch`/`updateKnockback`/`unstickEnemy` only read it. Merchant quests unlock via `merchantTalks` and reuse `questEvent`. Why: impact balance stays in one tested place.
- Class skills/passives (level 1/4/8) and the level-gated Forbidden Zone (level seal, stronger enemies) are tuned in `class-passives.ts` (tested in `class-passives.test.ts`); engine reads `classMods` in speed, stamina, damage taken, crit chance/damage. Why: class balance stays in one tested place.
- Player rating (level + performance `adapt` + kill score) lives in `balance.ts` (tested in `balance.test.ts`); merchant quests/rewards, AI advice and Forbidden Zone enemies scale with `Game.rating`, and zone enemies re-scale when a Forbidden door opens. The zone has two level-sealed doors linking two rooms. Why: balance follows the player, not only zone level.
- XP curve, per-source XP log labels and damage/merchant XP live in `xp.ts` (tested in `xp.test.ts`); `gainXp(n, source)` records `Game.xpLog`; `xpToReach` follows `xpForLevel`. Why: leveling pace is tuned in one tested place.
- Score merits/penalties, grades, recap text and the grade-based gold bonus live in `score.ts` (`ScoreKeeper`, tested in `score.test.ts`); engine only calls `score.gain/add/mech/tick` at events, `finishFloor` shows the recap (mode `recap`) before `continueFloor`, and run-end bonuses persist in localStorage `ars_anima_score_bonus`. Why: scoring stays in one tested place.
- Interaction always targets one thing: `focusPickup()` (nearest + in front) feeds both the prompt and `interact()`; `spreadPickups()` separates props, chests, merchant, benches and doors after floor generation; wandering merchants use `spawnWanderingMerchant()` (far rooms, long cooldown). Why: the prompt never lies about what E will do.
- Damage-cause labels/colors live in `damageCause` in combat-fx.ts (tested in combat-fx.test.ts); engine announces every player HP loss via `announceHurt`/`dotHurt` floats and records `lastHurt` for the death log. Why: no life loss without a visible reason, with DoT warnings throttled so they don't spam.
- Low profile (crouch/slide) hitbox and crawl-duct cell 11 live in `world-rules.ts` (`LOW_PROFILE`, `playerHitbox`, `projectileHitsPlayer`, tested in `lowprofile.test.ts`); engine uses `bodyH()` for player collision and auto-stays crouched inside ducts. Why: dodging by going low and crawl routes share one tested rule.
- Jump physics, ceiling height and reach helpers live in `JUMP`/`reachHeight`/`clampCeiling` (world-rules.ts); `platforming.test.ts` checks every generated ledge is reachable. Engine clamps the head to the ceiling and restores `safeX/safeY` if the player ends inside a wall or off-map. Why: platforming stays crossable and nobody escapes the level.

- EXPEDITION COLLAPSE phases, collapse timer/hazard scaling and the no-auto-extraction rule live in browser-safe `src/game/expedition.ts` (tested in `expedition.test.ts`); engine keeps live `exp`/`collapse`/`hazards` state, gates the boss on the map fragment and starts the collapse on boss death. Why: one tested place for the mandatory loop.
- PALIMPSESTOS data (documents, dictionary, library) lives in `palimpsest-data.ts`, rules/rewards/persistence (localStorage `ars_anima_palimpsestos`) in `palimpsest.ts` (tested in `palimpsest.test.ts`), UI in `PalimpsestPanel.tsx`; research requires sitting on a bench and no active collapse. Why: optional system separated from the main path and surviving death.
- Enemy name/state labels and life bars are collected in `Game.enemyLabels` during render and drawn crisp by GameView on a device-resolution overlay canvas matching the canvas cover crop. Why: 320x200 text was unreadable.
- Gamepad button/axis mapping lives in browser-safe `src/game/gamepad.ts` (tested in `gamepad.test.ts`); GameView polls it each frame and hides touch controls after the first deliberate pad/keyboard input, restoring them on screen touch. Why: OTG controllers replace on-screen controls only when actually used.
- The post-boss portal (collapse or not) spawns in the refuge at the free cell farthest from benches, decor and the merchant (`refugePortalSpot`, expedition.ts; random-room fallback); persistent floor darkening (map < boss, + per misread) is `dreadLevel` and is drawn on the minimap; debris impacts go through `debrisImpact` (area falloff, props, kegs, inner walls only). Extraction shows the "erased" mode before `finishFloor`. Why: border walls and the scoring flow must stay intact.
- A failed palimpsest confirmation calls `misreadSummon` (tuning in `MISREAD`, palimpsest.ts): one persistent mini-boss at a time, never during collapse, spawned outside the refuge. Why: optional puzzles get stakes without touching the main route or refuge safety.
- World effects (explosion flyers/gibs, flame flicker, plant sway, light-directed shadows, enemy glow cues, fountain puddles, speed lines) live in pure `world-fx.ts` (tested in `world-fx.test.ts`) and movement dust/drips in `motion-fx.ts`; engine only spawns/draws them. Why: visuals never alter damage, loot or AI.
- Run persistence lives in browser-safe `run-save.ts` (save of whitelisted `RUN_FIELDS`, player name/id, local run history, best/furthest run; tested in `run-save.test.ts`); `Game.saveRun/resumeRun/endRun` regenerate the saved floor on resume and only `die()`/victory end a run. Why: the run lasts until death without serializing level geometry.
- Chronicles use local `chronicles.ts` identities/events/titles/history and a replaceable ArchiveStore; simulation emits confirmed actions, puzzle state diffs emit discoveries, and scoring never changes grade rewards. Optional title AI only decorates already-unlocked deeds. Why: offline records stay real and online validation remains a separate future service.
- Enemy barks: event rules and fallback lines in `barks.ts`; AI repertoires come from `barks.functions.ts` per enemy kind, cached in localStorage; engine projects `barkLabels` and GameView draws bubbles. Why: presentation only, offline-safe, few AI calls.
- `carry.ts` owns weights, capacity and valuations; `subclasses.ts` owns saved subclass bonuses; `map-labels.ts` places labels without collisions. Why: consistent rules across engine and menus.
- Mobile gestures start movement only on the stick and camera look on every non-button surface, with independent captured pointer IDs; reload is a primary visible touch action. Why: transparent action layouts cannot block looking or force players into extra menus to reload.
- Weapon usage requirements live in browser-safe `weapon-access.ts`, shared by inventory and combat; merchant identity milestones live in `merchant-identity.ts` and their seen IDs extend the existing local legacy save. Why: one tested rule blocks all weapon activation paths and additive dialogue persists without changing commerce or adding remote player accounts.
- Aesthetic room themes, lights and decorative sidecars live in `scenery.ts`; engine builds tile lighting with geometry occlusion without gameplay RNG or pickups. Why: themed props, reflections and map details must not change combat, loot or movement.
- Alternate weapon geometry, timing, aimed-shot readiness and premium reward tiers live in browser-safe `weapon-modes.ts`; projectiles retain their own weapon/precision/hit history. Why: delayed hits preserve the shot's rules and cannot repeatedly damage the same enemy.
- `regions.ts` drives geometry, palettes, enemy pools and journey objectives, while `art.ts` references CDN crops from supplied boards; keep presentation separate from simulation so art changes do not alter rules.
- Emit semantic sound events from simulation and synthesize them in `audio.ts`, unlocked by user interaction; never emit audio from render loops because frames are not gameplay actions.
- Load gameplay sprites through `atlas.ts`, including the generated environmental sheet cropped by `props.ts`; tight bounds, original aspect ratios and support-height anchoring prevent stretched silhouettes.
- `animation.ts` creates reusable pixel-strip pose cycles; rendering selects poses from simulation state so animation never changes combat rules.
- HUD extras (compass, rotating round minimap with markers, contract tracker, status list) read only from `Game.snapshot()`/live game state. Why: presentation never mutates rules.
- Floating-paper HUD uses stacked edge layouts and snapshot-driven damage/weather surfaces; platform controls are detected after mounting. Why: keeps combat visible and avoids desktop touch controls or overlapping notes.
- World volume definitions and combo XP/door conditions live in browser-safe `world-rules.ts`; cells include 8 raised gallery and 9 overhead lintel. Use swept circle collision and per-pixel depth for actors/projectiles/rendering. Why: elevation, openings and cover must share geometry without replacing the raycaster.
- Actor movement uses `sweepActor` and overlap separation, while projectiles use geometry-only sweeps; `restRoom` protects vendor-room boundaries, spawning, damage and automatic one-use healing. Why: bodies collide without swallowing projectiles, and all enemy paths respect the refuge.
- Ranged shot profiles (speed, bursts, V-spread pellets, bounces, falloff, orb shockwaves), stamina costs, ally-death regeneration and hidden sanity live in browser-safe `combat-fx.ts`, covered by `combat-fx.test.ts`; all ranged weapons fire visible swept projectiles. Why: tuning stays in one tested place and fast shots cannot tunnel.
- Enemy evasion tuning lives in `EVADE`/`evadeChance` in `combat-fx.ts` (tested in `evade.test.ts`); engine only reads it. Why: dodge balance is adjusted in one tested place.
- Weapon wear/fail/forge rules live in browser-safe `weapon-wear.ts`; extra enemy roles and boss bullet-cloud patterns live in `enemy-roles.ts` (both tested in `wear-roles.test.ts`); engine only reads them. Why: balance tuning stays in one tested place.
- HUD art uses bundled `hud-restored` silhouettes with exterior-only chroma removal and opaque fills. Why: previous-project CDN pointers cannot load here; snapshot behavior stays unchanged.
- Floor-1 music, low-percussion beat detection and on-beat bonuses live in pure `beat.ts` (RHYTHM, tested in `beat.test.ts`); `audio.ts` decodes the song and exposes `onBeat()`, engine only reads `beatCheck()`. Why: rhythm tuning stays in one tested place.
- `genFloor` seeds Math.random with `floorSeed` (saved in RUN_FIELDS) and saves `pos`, so a resumed run rebuilds the same layout and position on any device. Why: location survives reload and device change.
- Weapon wiki sheets derive from generator rules in `weapon-wiki.ts`; game UI opens entries via `Game.openWiki(id)`. Why: wiki numbers never drift from real drops.
- Floor progress (alive enemies, opened chests, doors, seals, drops, boss/objective flags) is saved by `floor-save.ts` onto seed-rebuilt objects via generation-order `oid` (tested in `floor-save.test.ts`); results card/share is `RunCard.tsx`, floor ladder is `FloorProgressPanel.tsx`. Why: reloads resume the exact floor state.
- Class active skills (signature + alternates per class, subclass modifiers, charge by time/kills/hurt/damage) live in pure `class-skills.ts` (tested in `class-skills.test.ts`); the chosen skill per class persists in `ars_anima_skill_pick`. Enemy feuds and Tyrant ascension live in `feuds.ts` (tested in `feuds.test.ts`). Why: balance stays in tested modules; engine only applies effects.
- Active-skill run unlocks (`SKILL_UNLOCKS`, `skillUnlocked`) live in class-skills.ts; engine keeps per-run progress in `skillRun` (localStorage `ars_anima_skill_run`, reset on a new run) fed by `track()`, level-ups, floor changes and merchant purchases. Why: unlock rules stay pure and tested.
- Runtime-built HUD/weapon/skill sentences are translated by templates in `dynamic-phrases.ts` (English word order for weapon names) before the dictionary in auto-translate.ts. Why: dictionary word swaps read unnaturally for composed phrases.
- Boss fight rules (sealed lair doors, long time limit, rubble overtime, add summons, still/slow score drain, slam-jump with bouncing slow cloud + 2 adds) are tuned in `boss-arena.ts` (tested in `boss-arena.test.ts`); engine keeps live `arena` state and restores sealed cells on boss death. Why: boss pressure is tuned in one tested place.
- Player sprites use `player-sprite.ts`; `sprite-world.ts` renders all non-first-person views with Three.js instanced world volumes and upright loaded sprites from the existing single GameView loop. First person keeps the raycaster. Why: real depth without changing simulation or duplicating frame stepping.
- Falling debris uses pure swept height collision in `falling-rubble.ts` and marks each stone after a player hit. Why: descent contact matches the visible stone and cannot repeat damage each frame.
- `LiveProgress.tsx` opens the existing ProgressPanel without pausing; GameView snapshot refresh keeps character values current. Why: one progression sheet for live play and menus.
- Statistics store boss records per run/floor with time, damage and difficulty; floor-save restores spawned actors, terrain and arena/collapse state. Why: reload cannot erase a boss or its countdown and account snapshots retain records.
- GPU lighting never lets reflections replace floor/ceiling/sprite albedo (diffuse floor 0.85, ambient spec damped) and FXAA skips near depth edges. Why: floors went mirror-black and sprites blurred in first person.
- WebGPU G-buffer color texture needs RENDER_ATTACHMENT usage for copyExternalImageToTexture. Why: without it every texture rendered blank.
- Actor legibility (rim outline, pixel contrast, actor fog cap, external/aerial scale, 3D emissive) lives in browser-safe `sprite-legibility.ts` (tested); engine and sprite-world only wrap images with `legible()`; live canvases are flagged so tint/fog variants are redrawn. Why: visibility tuning stays presentation-only and in one place.
- P.A.R.I.A.H secret class: pure rules in `src/game/pariah.ts`, engine-facing runtime in `src/game/pariah-runtime.ts`; every engine hook is gated by `isPariah(cls.id)` and its weapons live in `PARIAH_WEAPONS`, never in `WEAPONS` — keeps other classes, loot, wiki and RNG untouched.
- Player hitbox is silhouette-based (`src/game/hitbox.ts`), shared by the single-player engine and the PvP sim so both judge hits identically.
